05Usability analysis

Written from the point of view of a shopper who wants to get from "I'd like to pick up some flower at Melrose" to "order placed, points earned" with as little effort as possible. Steps follow the order a shopper meets them. Each point says what slows the shopper down and gives a concrete fix.

1. Arrive

  • Two pop-ups before the first product. A new visitor gets the age gate plus the "Sign Up For VIP Deals" form layered on top of it. The visitor has to close a marketing form before they can even confirm their age; the pop-up sits on top of the YES button (M-6). Fix: show the age gate alone. Offer VIP sign-up later, e.g. after the first add to bag or in the footer.
  • The cookie text is not accurate. The age gate says "we never collect any personal data", yet ad-tech cookies (doubleclick, quantserve, sitescout, exelator, crwdcntrl) are already set before YES is pressed, and there is no accept/decline choice (M-15). Fix: load trackers only after consent and give a clear accept/decline choice. At the very least, reword the notice so it matches what the site does.
  • Slow first paint. Only 10 of 96 pages finished loading in under 3 s in the first sample; the median is about 3.2 s (M-12). Fix: defer the 29 plugin scripts and third-party tags, and load only the scripts each page needs.

2. Choose a store

  • Nothing can be bought until a store is chosen, and the site doesn't say so. Before choosing, every card says "Check Availability", and some prices change once a store is picked: 11 of 17 products changed price, e.g. Strawberry Jam $7.94 to $11.91 (M-1). The shopper sees one price and then pays another. Fix: ask for the store on arrival, or label pre-store prices as "from" prices and say that prices vary by store.
  • Store names look like code. The picker lists "The-Cure-Company-Melrose" and "CCC-by-The-Cure-Company" (L-2). Fix: show readable names ("The Cure Company - Melrose") with the address and opening hours.

3. Find

  • No sort. There is no way to sort by price, potency or newest on category or search pages (M-3). A price-conscious shopper has to scroll through 315 flower products. Fix: add a sort menu (price low-high, high-low, THC, newest).
  • Filters are messy. "Pre-Rolls" and "Pre-rolls", "Pre-Roll" and "Pre-Rolls" appear as separate subcategories. Other filters include "Root category" and a brand called "Custom Brand". Sizes are not in numeric order, and pre-rolls turn up under Flower (M-10). Filtering does work (Indica 315 to 62). Fix: clean the category and brand data at the source and sort sizes numerically.
  • Mobile menu has no Shop link. The hamburger menu lists Brand, Locations, Genetics, Wholesale, About Us and Contact Us, but not Shop, and BRAND points at the bare domain, which redirects (M-7). Fix: put Shop first in the menu and link BRAND to the www URL.
  • Tapping a filter's name does nothing on mobile. On mobile only the small checkbox toggles; tapping the word "Indica" does nothing (L-7). Fix: wrap each checkbox and its text in a <label>.
  • Search works. "alien og" found 10 results, and a nonsense query shows "No Products Found". The empty state still lists brand filters, though.

4. Choose a product

  • The product page leaves out what cannabis shoppers compare on. There is no size or weight picker, no THC/CBD percentage and no quantity selector before adding. Two cards can share one name at different prices with no size shown, e.g. "Alien OG - Smalls" at $79.41 and $142.94 (M-11, M-10). Fix: show weight, potency and strain type near the price, and one product with a size picker instead of look-alike cards.
  • Broken images. 6 product images return 403 from S3 (M-13), and related products are a generic list. Fix: re-upload or re-permission the images and show a placeholder when one fails.

5. Bag

  • The bag total doesn't say what it is. After adding a $79.41 item, the bottom bar shows "$99.99" with no label. That figure is the total including sales and excise tax, and the product page never mentions tax (M-2). Fix: label it "Total incl. tax $99.99", and add "plus sales and excise tax" under the product price.
  • Accessibility button on top of Checkout. On mobile the UserWay icon sits on the right end of the sticky Checkout button (M-8). Fix: move the widget up or to the left on pages with the sticky bar.
  • Your bag disappears when you log in. A guest who fills the bag and then logs in (as checkout invites) finds it empty (H-1). This is the most costly flaw for a shopper. Fix: merge the guest bag into the account bag on login.

6. Checkout

  • Promo codes only for members, and errors vanish. Guests see no promo field. Logged in, an invalid code shows "Invalid or expired promo code" for about 3 seconds and throws a script error (M-4). Fix: offer the field to everyone and keep the error next to the field until the code changes.
  • Step 2 is unclear. It asks for a Government ID and a Patient ID upload, yet Place Order becomes enabled at this step before either is given, and there is no pickup time or payment method on the page (M-5). A shopper can't tell what is required, when to pick up or how to pay. Fix: mark which fields are required (Patient ID for medical only), explain "pay in store at pickup" if that is the model, and offer a pickup time.
  • Hidden warning link. The Prop 65 warning link is black text on a black background (L-1). Fix: use a visible link colour.

7. Join, verify, rewards

  • Sign-up works but needs a phone number and a code. The code email's subject says "one time code to login" even at sign-up, and the welcome email arrives before the account is verified (L-6). Fix: separate sign-up and login templates, and send the welcome email after verification.
  • The wrong error for unverified accounts. Logging in before verifying shows "Invalid email or password" while the server says "Please verify your account first" (H-4). The shopper thinks they mistyped and may reset the password for nothing. Fix: show the server's message with a "send me a code" button.
  • Loyalty exists but members can't see it. The account is flagged as a loyalty member and the program has tiers, but the profile shows no points or tier, and /rewards and /loyalty are 404 (H-3). Fix: show the points balance and tier in the header and profile, and add a rewards page.
  • VIP deals need a phone and a signature. The only newsletter is the AlpineIQ VIP form. It requires first and last name, mobile phone, favourite store and a drawn signature, and its SMS/autodial consent box sits below the Sign Up button (M-6). Most shoppers will stop at the phone field. Fix: email-only sign-up with SMS as a separate opt-in, and put the consent box above the button.

8. Account security

  • Password reset links never expire and can be reused (SEC-1). A reset link keeps working after it has been used, even hours later, and the token has no expiry. Anyone who gets hold of an old reset email (a shared inbox, forwarded mail) can take over the account. The link in the email also has no https:// (H-2), so some mail apps treat it as a relative link and it fails.

Top 5 fixes, ranked by impact on a shopper

  1. Keep the bag on login (H-1): merge the guest bag into the account.
  2. Make reset links single-use and expiring, and add https:// to the link (SEC-1, H-2).
  3. Show loyalty points and tier to members (H-3), and give unverified users the right message (H-4).
  4. Make the price honest from the first view: choose the store up front or mark prices as varying by store, and label the bag total as including tax (M-1, M-2).
  5. Make checkout step 2 clear: required fields, pickup time, how payment works, and Place Order enabled only when ready (M-5), plus a promo field for everyone (M-4).
